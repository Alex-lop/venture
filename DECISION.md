**FATAL — 1. The flagship artifact disproves B's buyer thesis, and nobody read it by column.**

**FATAL — 2. The reshaped B wedge has a free, statewide, statutory substitute that the B1 verifier already named and DECISION.md did not answer.**
M.G.L. c.131 §40 requires that a conservation-commission hearing on a Notice of Intent be noticed **"by publication in a newspaper of general circulation in the city or town where the activity is proposed,"** at **"the expense of the applicant,"** **"not less than five days prior to such hearing"** (hearing due **"within twenty-one days of the receipt of said notice"**) — https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXIX/Chapter131/Section40. Every NOI/RDA row in the sample is therefore a legally-mandated newspaper legal ad. **masspublicnotices.org** aggregates every MA newspaper's legal notices — free (*"available to masspublicnotice.org users at no charge"*), searchable by **county, city/town, publication, date range and keyword**, rolling 12 months, self-described as covering *"foreclosures, **hearings**, advertisements for bids…that are legally required to be published"* — https://www.masspublicnotices.org/. The b1 verifier called this *"The single largest omission: it is a free substitute for the core wedge, not just the RFP leg."* DECISION.md's answer was to move from c.40A §11 (zoning, 14-day notice) to conservation filings — which c.131 §40 puts in the same free aggregator. Nobody ran the keyword search. It costs ten minutes and it is the single cheapest thing that could kill B.

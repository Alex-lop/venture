- A1 is dead on the evidence (see its "Live sweep results"). B4, C1-as-pitched, the four B6 niches, and most round-2 dev-tool ideas were killed by free incumbents that shipped in the last eight weeks.
- **Final calls after the red team and its follow-ups:** A (agent-readiness) survives only as an unpriced experiment; B1 and B′ are killed; C is the legal hedge. See `../DECISION.md` v3.
- The surviving shape is consistent across 24 dossiers: **moats are local and physical (Boston, Massachusetts town-level data, in-person), or money changes hands on delivery (services).** Dev-tool ideas a solo developer can build in eight weeks have already been built by six solo developers with zero stars.
